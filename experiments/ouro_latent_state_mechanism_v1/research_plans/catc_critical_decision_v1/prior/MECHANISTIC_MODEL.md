# Evidence-supported mechanistic model

A reproducible conditional, distributed boundary-to-late-update-to-readout path is supported. K2 makes answer-relevant states more accessible to the original fixed readouts. Boundary-conditioned late attention differences participate in natural K2 corrections; downstream effects can be tested through separate L18 residual and attention branches. These interventions establish computational participation on specified receivers, not semantic information origin, a single writer, universal necessity/sufficiency or a percentage of all reasoning explained.

Nonblocked cases, failed single-branch recoveries, negative exact contrasts and ties delimit the account. AB1 boundary specificity failures remain failures despite narrower B2 effects.

An inference-time principle is to preserve compatibility between recurrent context updates and both downstream readout branches, and test path-sensitive behavior instead of hidden-distance alone. A label-free natural utility/control signal is speculative. Offline gold-stratified causal analysis neither authorizes label-dependent inference nor establishes an accuracy-improving controller.
