# Train- and validation-informed P/Q/R eight-corner plan

Exploratory plan frozen after inspection of train and validation ROUND_2 effects and before revealing any test ROUND_2 intervention effect. This is not the original ROUND_2 preregistration and is not an independent blinded validation. The full implementation and numerical gates remain subject to Astra review; this document does not authorize a new GPU run.

P is the fixed 25-shot prefix, Q is the current-question prompt excluding the actual readout predecessor, and R is the actual readout predecessor. For each real request, use original metadata and verified boundary ledger, assert P/Q/R are disjoint and cover all effective tokens, and reject boundary-crossing or zero-offset tokens. Capture K1 and K2 full-sequence states independently for the same question and input at physical layer 12 output (index 11); never share prefix states across questions.

Let F_pqr be the fixed K1-strongest-wrong gold log-probability gap for the condition whose P, Q and R come respectively from Kp, Kq and Kr. Reuse the four independently audited original ROUND_2 conditions F111, F112, F221 and F222. Add all four F122, F212, F121 and F211, for eight corners. For each R background r in {1,2}, report P contribution at Q2 = F22r−F12r and at Q1 = F21r−F11r; Q contribution at P2 = F22r−F21r and at P1 = F12r−F11r; interaction I_r=F22r−F12r−F21r+F11r and the background difference I_2−I_1. In R2, F222−F112 must reproduce the old audited C|R2 effect; check both decompositions F222−F112=(F222−F122)+(F122−F112) and F222−F112=(F222−F212)+(F212−F112), accounting for floating-point arithmetic separately from strict model-output anchors.

All four new conditions cover every question in train, validation and test; no conditions or cases selected using intermediate effects. Preserve original train 25-example overlap and no-demo denominators, all individual data, negative/zero effects, top ties and full candidate scores. Predefine full-question atomic outputs, true BF16 full-vocabulary logits for every condition and candidate, strict same-device independent replay, input/metadata/source/manifest bindings, complete coverage ledger, bidirectional input restoration and identity anchors and fault handling before execution. Ensure original endpoint outputs have exact verified provenance. Different P and Q token counts limit interpretations to region-level replacements, not per-token importance or a unique mechanism. Mixed states may be out of distribution.
