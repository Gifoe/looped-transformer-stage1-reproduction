# Final mechanistic model

**Status:** E1/E2 and the specified E4/E5 programs are audited. E3 parent attention-bundle Train/Validation and 64-case E/L factor discovery are audited. E3 L10–L16 nested confirmation passed its six frozen tests in Train and Validation; the final Astra scientific review is complete. This document is a Terra synthesis of cited completed evidence, not a new Astra-signed review.

The best-supported account is a set of conditional state-to-readout computations, not a complete causal origin for the natural loop gain. Under an artificial fixed K1 receiver, the L18 SDPA finite-difference interaction has a transferable effect on candidate competition; later computation has an additional, sometimes opposing nonlinearity. Natural K2 receiver interventions independently support residual/suffix effects and an attention-mediated contribution for a subset. Q-region L9 input perturbation affects R-state updates across L10–L16, but discovery alone cannot establish a unique writer, information origin, or natural path necessity.

- **E1 / local causal:** the terminal candidate gap interaction decomposes exactly into the measured difference between S22 and the additive SDPA intervention and the residual suffix non-additivity. In natural-rescue Test questions (N=378), Q1/Q2 means are +0.393794/+0.499311 for the removal effect and −0.054481/−0.048060 for the suffix term. The latter includes the entire downstream suffix. Train damage Q1 violates its frozen negative-direction prediction (+0.000672; N=31); preserve this failure.
- **E2 / descriptive and local intervention:** the fixed linear probe can decode information from R states, but decodability alone does not imply model use. Its global rank-4 span includes an extra non-offered E-axis accounting for a median 95.77% of projected-change energy; the offered-candidate rank-3 span is a sensitivity control, not a proven answer subspace. E2.3 rollback effects differ from unrelated controls but the complement/full and probe-span perturbations have unequal doses. E2.4 compares actual mixed residual/attention states; it is not an explained percentage.
- **E3 / local intervention and conditional discovery:** the same-site 4090 Q9-to-R L9–L16 attention bundle passes six frozen Train1030 and Validation299 tests; the discovery partition E=L9 fails natural-context loss and norm-loss direction, while L=L10–L16 passes all six exploratory checks. L nested confirmation passed all six frozen criteria separately in Train and Validation. The Q9 splice and R-bundle influence on final scores are causal for the specified artificial interventions. L18 x/a/SDPA distances are diagnostics, not an independent mediation cut, and no single writer has been isolated. L9 endpoint identity establishes correct implementation, not first information creation.
- **E4 / natural-receiver controlled mediation:** under prior-state R rollback, actual native-attention restoration recovers some lost natural corrections. In the frozen 128 confirmation at K2, 6 of 9 blocked natural corrections recover; only 2 meet all four controls and tie-free specificity. Its mean gap restoration +0.040446 has a 95% question-bootstrap interval [−0.0160,+0.0970], so this is not a general positive mean claim. Earlier 299 and later128 remain separate cohorts.
- **E5 / later loops:** interventions still alter states at K3/K4, but natural risk sets shrink and average restoration is negative in both evaluated cohorts. The 128 K4 native correct count falls from 110 at K3 to 107. Different natural perturbation magnitudes prevent an equal-dose sensitivity or convergence claim.

E4/E5 tested distinct R/attention interventions; they neither directly validate the new E3 L pathway nor establish its behavior at K3/K4. More detailed evidence and negative cases are indexed in `FINAL_RESEARCH_REPORT.md`, `CAUSAL_EVIDENCE_MAP.md`, and `COUNTEREXAMPLES.md`. See sibling E1_E2_A6000 and E4_E5_4090 releases for raw compact evidence and exact audit chains.

**Q-region boundary:** Q denotes the entire current-input token region after the fixed few-shot prefix and before the final R token, including the question, answer options and associated formatting; it is not an isolated semantic question-stem source.

**Existing parent L18 key-edge intervention:** The earlier full attention bundle also tested P/PK/P+A/P+A+key on the same receiver. In a posthoc saved-score summary, natural-rescue Train337 key-at-P/key-at-P+A/interaction means were +0.494189/+0.610318/+0.116129; Validation104 were +0.437600/+0.569912/+0.132312. This is a real conditional key-to-R intervention in the parent experiment, not merely a state-distance diagnostic, but it is not a preregistered CI result or an independent mediation cut for the newer L-only intervention.
