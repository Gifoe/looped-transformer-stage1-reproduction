# Causal evidence map

| Evidence level | Observation or intervention | Supported | Not established |
|---|---|---|---|
| Descriptive | E2 saved R states, two loops×24 layers | the measured R representation changes | source or use of information |
| Decodability | fixed masked linear probes | candidates can be read out from these states under the selected fitting protocol | the model uses that probe span; the extra E-axis makes naive answer-subspace labeling unsound |
| Local causal | E1 S22 versus additive SDPA R injection | an effect of the specified finite-difference component in the fixed K1 receiver | natural K2 necessity or a pure MLP suffix |
| Local causal | E3 Q9 splice plus attention-branch reference deltas | specified Q-region and R-update interventions affect candidate gaps | a unique writer, earliest information origin, or a native path |
| Descriptive/diagnostic | E3 downstream L18 x/a/SDPA distances | measured states vary under the intervention | that a particular L18 state/edge mediates the final effect |
| Conditional natural-receiver mediation | E4 previous R block, actual blocked attention and native-attention restoration | controlled R-to-attention-to-output dependence in a subset | an explained fraction or proof that new E3 L updates are the mediator |
| Cross-depth conditional tests | E5 K3/K4 same-position interventions | receiver- and depth-dependent effects | equal-dose depth sensitivity, convergence, or direct validation of new E3 L path |

Frozen 4090 E3 discovery: E failed two of six criteria, L met six. Nested confirmation on 1030/299 passed all six frozen criteria in each split. Source detailed files live in sibling E1_E2_A6000/E4_E5_4090 and the staged E3 increment. Cohorts and hardware baselines are separate. Later results do not retroactively change frozen failures.

**Existing parent L18 key-edge intervention:** The earlier full attention bundle also tested P/PK/P+A/P+A+key on the same receiver. In a posthoc saved-score summary, natural-rescue Train337 key-at-P/key-at-P+A/interaction means were +0.494189/+0.610318/+0.116129; Validation104 were +0.437600/+0.569912/+0.132312. This is a real conditional key-to-R intervention in the parent experiment, not merely a state-distance diagnostic, but it is not a preregistered CI result or an independent mediation cut for the newer L-only intervention.
