## L9 / E negative result and mediation boundary

The fixed-three technical cases bridge the actual L9 Q-region joint K/V swap to the same-receiver E-only local increment and full-logit suffix. The discovery analysis finds that P+E recovers the tested station's R post-MLP state but has little average decision effect; N−E has negative mean loss in the natural context. The technical endpoint proves that the L9 read operation was implemented as intended, not that L9 first created the information. E's failed pre-frozen loss directions are retained alongside L's discovery effects.

The perturbations establish the effects of their specified Q9 splice and L10–L16 R attention-update bundle on the final candidate gap in the tested receiver. The saved L18 residual/attention state distances and projections are diagnostics; they do not by themselves establish that the final effect is mediated uniquely by a particular L18 edge. The existing E4/E5 studies were conducted under their own interventions and must not be presented as independent validation of this new L path. L-only nested confirmation meets all six frozen criteria, but this does not close the downstream mediation or initial-information-origin gaps.

**Q-region boundary:** Q denotes the entire current-input token region after the fixed few-shot prefix and before the final R token, including the question, answer options and associated formatting; it is not an isolated semantic question-stem source.

## Receipt naming

`PENDING.json` is an immutable historical receipt written when collection finished and independent auditing was still pending. Its filename and original status are retained for provenance; they do not mean a later completed audit is still running. For each namespace, current completion is established by matching that receipt's SHA against the corresponding successful `CUDA_REPLAY.json` and `INDEPENDENT_CPU_MATH_AUDIT.json`, and, for reported findings, the final linked analysis. A missing or mismatched later receipt must not be treated as completed. Historical receipts are never rewritten to reflect newer status.

The frozen 4090 source snapshot README is preserved as originally captured and its then-PENDING status is historical; the completed L-only receipts and analysis are in `results/conditional_l_holdout/`.
