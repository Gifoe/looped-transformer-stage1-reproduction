# Mechanism counterexamples

Prior eight-corner Validation Q1/K1 V-only produced 7 corrections and 8 harms, net −1 despite a positive mean gap. Example Mercury_7138688 improved mean gold-vs-wrong gap while dynamic strongest-wrong margin crossed from positive to negative and the decision became wrong.

E1 stratified discovery64: in the natural rescue group ADD caused four harms in each query background, while the unrelated matched-delta control caused three and five harms respectively. This prevents a claim that ADD removal has already shown decision-level specificity. Four groups have only 16 cases each and cannot be treated as representative cohort proportions.

Uniform SDPA scaling followed by bias-free o_proj and local RMS normalization can be approximately cancelled (apart from epsilon and finite precision); ADD≈ADD_NORM does not alone prove a pure direction mechanism.
