For any terminal gap G, key-route effects are G00-G01 and G10-G11. Other producer-background effects are G00-G10 and G01-G11. Interaction is G00-G10-G01+G11. Report each conditional effect and its individual distribution; do not turn these into mediation percentages or assume additivity. Preserve actual K_Q change and R-state change as diagnostics, not final scores.

A transported-key effect with a matching restoration effect supports a conditional K_Q route. Weak transport or ineffective restoration leaves bypass/compatibility explanations open even if the complete producer perturbation is strong. Harm confined to large hybrid changes is not sufficient to establish a natural necessary path. An effect at the target-site anchor alone is not an upstream finding.
