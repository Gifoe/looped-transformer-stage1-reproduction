# R-side writer candidate: decision brief, not a selected writer

R producer64 used the pre-frozen, outcome-balanced Train sample (16 per natural K1→K2 group), same-question natural K1 R donor into natural K2 at physical inputs 0/8/16 (L1/L9/L17), with physical17/L18 direct rollback as an audited target anchor. Every cut's R-only intervention and L18 non-R identity were verified on the actual forward; an independent bare-hook CUDA replay matched all 192 full logits, local attention outputs, scores and input hashes. CPU source/target anchor audit and independently recomputed saved scores passed. This is a targeted cut experiment, not a matched-dose perturbation or a localized writer intervention.

For mean gold−allwrong terminal loss from native K2 on the balanced 64, L1/L9/L17 cuts give 1.608/0.531/1.064; direct L18 input rollback gives 1.092. In the 16 native-rescue cases, the corresponding losses are 2.395/1.038/1.999 and decision damage 5/1/5. In 16 native-damage cases L17 loss is −0.180 and the cut rescues 5. L18 actual x_R change L2 averaged 8.869/3.940/4.276 for L1/L9/L17. The L9 endpoint is less disrupted than direct L18 rollback for this fixed sample, but is not hidden-state recovery, an explained fraction, or a measurement of importance: cut inputs have unmatched perturbation norms, nonlinear dynamics, and potentially different distributions.

**Candidate for a falsifiable next design:** examine the coarse L9-input→L17-input interval as an interval hypothesis, then separately test attention versus MLP updates at R within a *small set of predeclared intermediate boundaries*. This is a proposal for scientific review, not a frozen producer, writer localization, or authorization for another scan. Compare at least (1) recurrent computations between these boundaries rebuild the target-relevant R state, versus (2) the smaller terminal loss is due to different off-manifold perturbation magnitude/direction and downstream compensation. A third explanation is redundant pathways: an individual component may have little rollback effect while a joint cut matters. Because x_R changes Q, its local K/V and residual simultaneously, no result here is a pure-query mechanism.

Before any held-out outcome, freeze exact component/site/loop/readout, donor/receiver identity, cutoff plan, direction of predicted effects, matched-norm and unrelated controls at that same cut, identity, recovery/rollback, and quantitative treatment of interactions and invalid zero norms. Evaluate actual target x_R and attention/localnorm changes plus final allwrong/fixedwrong/dynamic candidate gaps and prediction changes; do not replace these with probe accuracy or a diagnostic LM-head lens. Tests that distinguish the hypotheses include a target-state transfer/restore under a matched perturbation and matched-dose unrelated direction; include counterexamples and interactions. A full-grid layer/head scan is out of scope.

Holdout route: remaining Train non-demo 1030 and Validation299 after a plan is frozen, with prior E1/ARC and multiple project results already exposed, hence not a new independent blind test. The initial 64 informed candidate/plan, so no 64-derived cut or sign may be presented as prespecified validation. If matched controls or held-out effects do not support the interval, record this as a negative result rather than moving boundaries after inspection. Q-key results have not yet been integrated and may challenge this proposal.
