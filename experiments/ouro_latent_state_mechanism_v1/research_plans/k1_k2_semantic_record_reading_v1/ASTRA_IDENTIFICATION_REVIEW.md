## Single primary configuration and rationale
Use the TWO WHOLE EQUAL-LENGTH MAPPING RECORDS as the swapped source blocks, all heads together, actual post-RoPE K and actual V. The records must have equal token length and include their complete relation text and terminating delimiter. Avoid a terminal-token carrier assumption: a single token can miss distributed record information, repeating the earlier failed single-edge pattern. Whole-record permutation tests record-level pairing, not a particular word or head.

Receivers are ALL H positions strictly after the second complete mapping record, including query/options/readout R. K2 intervention window is L9–16 inclusive, used as one bundle. This is the same coarse receiver extent supported by old C, now in a natural background with a semantic permutation. Do not select a question-mark token or R alone: neither is established as the unique receiver of this synthetic task's relation information. R supplies the final measurement, not a localization claim. The price is an explicitly broad causal claim; later localization is a separate study. There is no outcome-driven fallback receiver/window.

Same intervention in K1 is a matched round comparator. It measures change in participation of semantic record rereading between natural round states, NOT absence of information in K1. K1 and K2 input states legitimately differ; that is part of the phenomenon, not proof of newly acquired information.

## Primary estimands and statistical family
Per input at round r, define opposite-minus-selected offered raw-logit gap using that input's semantic label coding. Treatment effect is that gap after the K-only bundle minus native gap, measured at the END OF THE SAME ROUND. V-only is an equivalence control, not independent evidence. K1 intervention's final K2 output is secondary because it has an extra round of downstream computation.

The semantic prediction is that swapping record pairing shifts preference toward the opposite record endpoint, and that this shift tracks BOTH first-hop assignment and endpoint permutation when the queried object changes. To prevent cancellation hiding failures, preregister four primary K2 means stratified by first-hop×endpoint factor, each balanced over selector,label,order. Fifth primary contrast is K2-minus-K1 same-round mean treatment effect. Use base-group clustered bootstrap simultaneous95% intervals across these five quantities (Bonferroni99% individual intervals acceptable). Four K2 effects must each exceed the frozen practical/null threshold to support generality across tested semantic factors. Fifth contrast is assessed separately: positive establishes stronger involvement in K2; failure means no evidence of stronger second-round involvement, even if both rounds use the edge. Report unaggregated selectors and all counterexamples, all full-logit and accuracy outcomes.

Do NOT require averaged queried-record attention weight to be positive. Some heads suppress, projections change signs and generic heads dilute means. Diagnostic attention probabilities, per-head vectors and projected updates may be retained only after independently validating explicit attention against native SDPA. They explain observed operator effects but have no unvalidated positive-weight pass gate. Successful output-semantic permutation establishes causal pairing sensitivity; it does not by itself establish a particular positive attention-head algorithm.
