# Source-position decomposition of the selected query layer

This follow-up was specified after the selected-layer query effect was observed. It reuses the same confirmation cohort to decompose that effect, and does not count as a new independent replication. The source-position choice itself uses the original discovery stems only. At the selected layer and common answer pivot, extract the native attention weight row p and the row q from the exact all-head Q swap. All values are the recipient's. Split positions into preceding demonstrations, the first operand field, the second operand field, and the rule/answer scaffold including the naturally emitted prefix. Token-start offsets under the same tokenizer determine field membership.

With fixed recipient V for each head, the exact pre-output-projection readout difference is the sum of (q-p)V over the four disjoint segments. Use direct weight edits on the targeted query row to test one segment's contribution and the complement. These are algebraic path interventions and may have a total row mass different from one. The matched edit at the same positions preserves per-head sum and Euclidean norm of the changed weights, randomizing the zero-sum component. Full weight replay must reproduce the original all-head Q interchange at the predicted token. In discovery rank the four source segments by the smaller of mean correct gain in K1 and mean foil gain in K2, with earliest source order as the tie-breaker. Confirm the winner-only and complement operations, fully generated from the same sequence pivot, and record matched-control outputs. Count whether another earlier context region alone reproduces the effect through its complement.

Also decompose normalized attention into group masses and conditional within-group distributions. Test donor group masses with recipient conditional token weights, and recipient group masses with donor conditional token weights. Each of these two arms retains normalized rows. These distinguish between-region redistribution and within-region retargeting as causal manipulations. Treat exact counts and target-clustered intervals as within-cohort secondary evidence, and restrict conclusions to the manipulation, observed position groups and specific native error. This test does not assign a single internal neuron a symbolic borrow computation.
