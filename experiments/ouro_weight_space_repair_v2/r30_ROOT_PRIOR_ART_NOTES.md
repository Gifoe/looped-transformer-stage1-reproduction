# Root prior-art triage for a possible post-R30 design

Scope: primary-source abstracts and author project summaries checked on 2026-11-05 while the frozen R30 job was running. This is a limited screening note, not a novelty certificate or a new experiment authorization. No R30 effect was inspected for this note.

- [LoopCD: Loop-wise Contrastive Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24196): earlier-versus-final loop contrast already exists. Changing that contrast's coefficient, sign or chosen depth is insufficient by itself to establish novelty.
- [Decoding Looped Transformers Better for (Almost) Free](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02185): both logit-space and hidden-state recurrent contrasts are existing approaches. Merely moving an unchanged contrast between these spaces is not a new mechanism.
- [Batch Calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17249), with its [author explanation](https://research.google/blog/batch-calibration-rethinking-calibration-for-in-context-learning-and-prompt-engineering/): estimating and removing contextual output bias is established. Its batch-estimation setting differs from this project's strictly per-prefix, future-target-free contract; importing test-batch statistics would require a different protocol and is not authorized here.
- [Rectifying Demonstration Shortcut in In-Context Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09488): demonstration-aware calibration and separating pretrained semantic priors from demonstrated mappings are established problem formulations. A new source intervention needs a measurable distinction from this literature.
- [Function Vectors in Large Language Models](https://functions.baulab.info/): task-conditioned attention-head outputs and causal task-vector interventions are prior art. The project's finite R27 value-channel result establishes transport on its own synthetic screen, not novel discovery of task vectors or an ARC utility guarantee.
- [Steering Llama 2 via Contrastive Activation Addition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06681): contrastive residual activation differences and additive intervention are established. A changed vector construction still needs a reason that its direction carries useful evidence rather than just affecting output.

These sources do not by themselves rule out a new prefix-specific recurrent mechanism. A useful next proposal must state what earlier negative experiments actually falsify, identify a distinct causal prediction, and give one small discriminating test. It must not present generic causal influence, a direction norm, a vocabulary-null comparison, or a reused 24-question gain as proof of accuracy generalization.

Execution constraints remain unchanged: frozen weights, no controller training, same folder, ARC only, no test tuning, no extra GPU allocation, no modification of public/frozen artifacts. Any changed numerical execution protocol requires fresh matched baselines and separate fidelity review. R30 itself is not modified by these notes.
