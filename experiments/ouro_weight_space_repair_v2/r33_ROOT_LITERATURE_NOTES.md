# R33 limited primary-source comparison

Primary papers were checked on 2026-11-05. These notes delimit the proposed claim; they do not establish novelty or transfer the papers' empirical results to Ouro/ARC.

[No-Worse Context-Aware Decoding, ACL Findings 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.473.pdf) compares with-context and no-context streams and routes using distribution divergence and confidence. Its exact output-preservation statement is conditional on selecting the no-context backoff branch under greedy decoding. This is not a guarantee that the branch is selected on every semantically neutral example or that every baseline-correct answer remains correct. The proposed R33 coordinate clamp has a different object: removing a shared portion of loop-induced log-probability gain at each vocabulary coordinate. Its local clamp inequality also does not guarantee final probability or answer preservation. An exact zero branch and a correct-answer guarantee must remain separate claims.

[CoCoA, EMNLP 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.348.pdf) adjusts context-aware contrastive decoding using confidence signals and distribution divergence. It motivates distinguishing useful context effects from generic shifts and discusses limitations of a divergence-only signal. This supports testing selectivity explicitly; it does not establish that agreement across three Ouro reference gains identifies unwanted bias. R33 must beat matched direction/scale controls and R6 in actual paired corrections rather than use lower norm, reference agreement or an adaptive rule as evidence of utility.

The inspected mechanisms are related prior art, not exhaustive nearest-neighbor retrieval for R33. Coordinatewise clipping, signed minima, calibration and contrastive products are established mathematical ingredients. The candidate's potentially useful distinction is the joint behavior of a current-prefix gain cap and fixed-reference consensus under recurrent scoring. Whether that interaction is effective or sufficiently differentiated remains unknown. No originality or performance claim follows from this screening note.
