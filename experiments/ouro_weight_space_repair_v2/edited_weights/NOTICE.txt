This experiment modifies selected MLP output columns and/or low-rank output
directions without gradient training. Each manifest identifies exactly which
BF16 parameters differ, the original and target hashes, and the magnitude.
These modified tensors are research derivatives, not an official upstream
checkpoint or an endorsement by ByteDance or MIT.

The original checkpoint, embeddings, tokenizer, LM head and normalization
parameters are not modified in their source files. Exact replacement patches
must be applied only to the pinned base verified by tensor SHA256.
