# R25 pre-execution pilot interpretation and resource decision

This decision is made before any R25 key, query, model execution or method result. It adopts the reasoning in `r25_PILOT_POWER_REVIEW.md`; the original v1/v2 design texts remain preserved. The method, references, controls, cohort, numerical invariants and full evaluation objective do not change.

The old strong 24-question screen remains intact and must be reported exactly as specified. Passing it is not a demonstration of generalization. Failing it also does not statistically rule out the desired small ARC improvement: under the review's simplified zero-damage model at +15/1172 absolute improvement, its two-rescue rule passes only about 3.60% of the time. That calculation is an illustrative assumption-dependent resource analysis, not the empirical power of this adaptive research history.

Root explicitly supersedes the earlier self-imposed rule that only a strong pilot pass may enter a budget review. There are now three predeclared interpretations, evaluated once only after complete, numerically valid output:

1. **Strong screen passed:** all original R25 gates pass. Proceed only to a concrete cost and full-chain budget review.
2. **Non-pass eligible for resource review:** all input/numerical/parity/parameter/completion checks pass, no unmatched control positions, primary net versus R6 is nonnegative, and all pilot R6-unique-versus-Fixed successes are retained. This is not a positive efficacy result or a method pass. It permits reviewing whether a complete 576-question controlled development evaluation is justified and affordable. Every original control and sensitivity result must still be disclosed, including failures.
3. **No continuation under this stage:** otherwise close this exact pilot after publishing the observed outcome. Do not infer that all small effects or all memory methods are absent.

Neither interpretation 1 nor 2 authorizes a further model job, a smaller confirmation set, a controller, a new candidate, a changed metric or an automatic budget increase. The original full 576 development, **517 confirmation**, 299 validation and 1,172 ARC evaluation requirements remain intact. No 64-question substitute is accepted. The full-chain estimate already exceeds the remaining original cap before adding the new retrieval cost.

After a qualifying complete pilot, Root must use measured key establishment, audit, Q head, reference forward/head, retrieval and check timings to present a concrete complete-evaluation budget. A request under interpretation 2 must explicitly say that efficacy is unproven; it cannot be described as expansion of a successful method. Until that review and any necessary user budget authorization, no additional GPU work is allowed. The sole initial job remains capped at 300 seconds and all actual time counts against the original 21,600-second limit.

This change reduces an unjustified statistical interpretation of a tiny pilot; it does not lower the actual research objective or turn a failed screen into success.
