# Which K2 attention inputs carry the causal first-operand effect

This stage is conditional on the independent operand recombination gate. Select up to 24 discovery and 48 confirmation stems from the exact two-way prefix-transfer stems, with one fixed random sample per split. If fewer are eligible, retain each. The selection conditions on a measured causal prefix effect, so conclusions apply to that population. Use original prompt pairs, dose, donor activations, native weights and exact free generation from the operand experiment. Recompute each no-intervention and prefix-only path and replay the stored complete sequences.

Let S be the contiguous copied prefix positions. At each physical layer of K2, record the native recipient K after rotation and V before grouped-query broadcast from a separate native K2 forward at the same generated continuation. In the hybrid K2 forward restore those native projections only at S. Keep all queries and all other positions in their hybrid state. Test K restoration, V restoration and both. In each matched control restore a synthetic vector norm- and angle-matched to the natural restoration at the same layer and positions, using the flattened heads per position. Match relative to the actually perturbed projection immediately before each adaptive intervention. Treatment keeps the K1 unembedding readout fixed.

Report exact recipient restoration, counterfactual retention and other outputs independently. Compare whole two-way exact recipient restoration of the K and V restorations to their own controls over the frozen confirmation sample. Use 97.5% paired bootstrap intervals for those two tests. As structural validation, restoring both K and V at the graft positions through all layers must reproduce the native readout, since these are the only routes from a changed causal prefix into the completely untouched suffix. This joint test is an operator consistency check and carries no independent attribution to K or V in isolation. Report K/V effects as path interventions spanning all layers and treat single-head, layer and MLP identities as unresolved.
