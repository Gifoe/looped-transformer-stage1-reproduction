# Opposite corrections from the same first-pass answer

Eligible means both complete K1 outputs equal precisely that midpoint. A direct reversal requires that both complete K2 outputs equal their respective true integers. Every sampled pair and generated sequence is reported.

The universe is 300 midpoint integers from 400 through 699. For each midpoint sample eight stems, with four varying the first operand and four varying the second. Balance the two feasible lower-digit sum branches (carrying or not carrying into the hundreds) when possible. Require at least 12 distinct eligible midpoint bins, at least 12 exact direct reversals, paired two-sided 95% percentile lower endpoint above zero, and positive measured excess in each changed-operand orientation with all denominators stated.
