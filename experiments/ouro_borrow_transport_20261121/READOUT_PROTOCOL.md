It moves an actual L17 effect induced by L14 intervention, instead of reapplying a donor query on recipient K and V. Register it before reading the primary transport outputs. Run when native matching passes. It does not count as an independent replication of the query-transport claim.

Unavailable basis arms are reported unchanged and left in the denominator. Match the projection's norm and its dot products against the original readout and original total change.
