# Narrow prior-art screen

Primary sources checked on 2026-10-30. This is a screening pass, not a systematic novelty certification. Entries describe authors' claims, not independently reproduced measurements.

- [Neural Pairing README at the audited commit](https://github.com/SauersML/neural-pairing/blob/7e7fcc401a8243f94bca94070db0993d64277874/README.md): already reports QA disagreement damage, asymmetric benefits, late-steering attenuation, write-strength interventions and steering-reading training. Merely rediscovering negative transfer, attenuation or supervision sensitivity is insufficient novelty.
- [When Does Latent Communication Pay?](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04893v2): full HTML inspected for audit design/scope. Uses mismatched, zero and matched-random relay controls, distinguishes private-information necessity from ordinary benchmark gains, and compares delivered channels. It rules out presenting the control battery or transfer-versus-reasoning distinction as a new idea. Its cache-relay systems differ from Neural Pairing's simultaneous residual bridges.
- [StateBridge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13317): primary abstract verified. Proposes closed-form alignment with norm calibration and vocabulary anchoring. Alignment as a remedy is established prior work; its final-state continuous-prefix design differs from this bridge.
- [When Upstream Messages Override Correct Answers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36855): primary abstract verified. Studies fixed receiver evidence under original/opposite upstream messages and wrong-answer adoption. Behavioral override or imitation alone is not a novel discovery. Its message intervention does not establish where Neural Pairing's hidden states fail.
- [Portable Semantics, Private Dialects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11365): primary abstract verified. Tests frozen interoperability and negative transfer in a restricted 17-state setting. Do not generalize its interface-learning results directly to these pretrained 4B models.

No claim that recurrent computation fixes these systems follows from this screen. Full-paper and code comparison is still required before declaring either focused follow-up novel.

## Mechanism-stage refresh, 2026-10-30

The full HTML of the above four arXiv papers was opened during the mechanism audit; targeted sections were checked for the actual intervention boundary and limitations. The upstream-message study explicitly leaves internal evidence replacement versus excessive weighting unresolved. Its textual upstream-message design does not establish a Neural Pairing residual-bridge circuit. Portable Semantics localizes a specific instruction-execution failure through a source-span control in its17-state setting; it already invalidates any broad claim that distinguishing carriage from use is new.

- [Do Latent Channels Actually Communicate?](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26773v1), Zhang and Emu: primary full HTML inspected. Separates presence, example identity and other-agent value using controlled boundary substitutions on Qwen3-4B/8B and natural benchmarks. Content-versus-presence decomposition and message replacements are established audit ideas, not novelty here.
- [Hidden Messages Between Diffusion Language Model Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.28905v1), Gazzaev et al.: primary full HTML opened. Uses content substitutions and temporal intervention in diffusion trajectories, including distributed QASC. Exchange timing versus frequency already has direct experimental treatment; our small layer-lesion heatmap is not the first timing audit.
- [CacheBack, author project page](https://daplab.cs.columbia.edu/projects/cacheback/): verified primary description. Receiver-conditioned attention selects cached sender positions in split-context tasks. Receiver-conditioned selection and efficient cache communication are prior design choices. This is not an intervention study of reverse-path-induced harm in simultaneous residual bridges.

Narrow candidate for this checkpoint: a sender can keep its final answer correct while its communication-conditioned states cause a receiver error; same-question K/V patches with factual receiver-write norms ask whether the reverse path changes a causally harmful direction beyond magnitude. The screen did not locate that exact8-depth direction/norm intervention in these inspected sources, but this is not evidence of worldwide priority. Without independent repeated cases, upstream-precision robustness and a concrete semantic counterfactual, describe it as a localized candidate rather than an established novel mechanism.
