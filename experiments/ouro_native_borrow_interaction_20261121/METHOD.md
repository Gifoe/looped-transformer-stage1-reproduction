To have the same joint remainder without borrow its two edits sum to a smaller value. This numeric control and the activation geometry controls answer different questions.

On each original prompt capture the terminal readout state at the beginning of physical L17 at the same naturally emitted decision prefix. Let r_{s,t,k} be that state for corner s,t and loop k. The held-out signed perturbation is

```text

K1 receiving readout: r_11,1 + D
K2 receiving readout: r_11,2 - D
```

These operations swap the measured interaction increment while leaving the receiving computation and token stream in their natural phases. Source I_2 includes the correct joint's own representation, so same-example interventions can also carry that candidate. The two recipient baseline vectors define a plane. The geometric control for D keeps its projections on both plane axes and its magnitude while randomly changing the perpendicular direction, preserving the perturbation's final receiving-vector norms. The alternate safe-quartet treatment is dose matched. Cross-example interventions additionally separate the donor and recipient's correct and error hundreds digits and report actual donor-answer collisions.
