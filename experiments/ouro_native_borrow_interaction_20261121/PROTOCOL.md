# Native four-corner interaction in recurrent borrow correction

Use the same frozen Ouro-1.4B Base revision 574fa66cb8bf5abdc979642d01cf2b79b16bfab1 and explicit reverse-subtraction prompt as the preceding experiments. Run native eager FP32 without cache, with full-vocabulary greedy generation and exact integer line scoring. All model choices, exact sets and selection rules are frozen before sampling any output.

Construct independent target pools for 12 discovery donor targets and 16 confirmation targets at each of five possible correct hundreds digits (2 through 6). Draw two stems per target. The borrow factorial fixes the original operand hundreds and uses each of two lower-part changes independently. A increases by d and B decreases by 100-d. Both single edits remain nonnegative in the low-part difference. The join alone crosses the borrow threshold and preserves the base remainder. For each stem construct a separate matched factorial without a borrow in any corner. Its joint has the identical full true answer and omission foil. Its low-digit edits have smaller sum by mathematical necessity. Retain separate controls for perturbation geometry. Whole operand pairs are disjoint from earlier decks and between every stem. Exact joint-target pools are disjoint across discovery and confirmation. The no-borrow quartet's first three prompts are opened only for state tracing. Benchmark all four borrowing corners and the matched safe joint under both depths.

Require at least 24 held-out native exact borrow omissions (K1 is y+100, K2 is y), at least 18 recovered target groups, and a positive 95% paired target-cluster lower endpoint versus the same-answer safe condition. Interpret the natural repair on every eligible joint, including earlier corner errors. Report the stricter subset in which all three single/no-edit controls were already correct at K1. A benchmark result is not proof of a latent borrow variable. The stage protocols freeze the exact cross-loop readout interventions, the cross-digit controls, and the L14-to-L17 path test independently. Keep hashes, full tokens, non-matches and harms.
