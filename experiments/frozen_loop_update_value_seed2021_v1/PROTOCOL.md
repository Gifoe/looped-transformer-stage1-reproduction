# Protocol, precommitted before new predictions

## Model, actions and scoring
Official ByteDance/Ouro-1.4B revision `574fa66cb8bf5abdc979642d01cf2b79b16bfab1`, BF16, SDPA, four native recurrent iterations, eval mode, no backbone updates. Use pinned lm-evaluation-harness 0.4.9 templates and pair encoding, no BOS, no silent truncation, full candidate likelihood. Report both raw accuracy and character-normalized accuracy; the latter is primary for all tasks here. All new tasks use zero-shot, as rendered by their official harness tasks. This does not repeat or replace the earlier ARC25-shot reproduction.

Four selectable actions: Rollback=z3, Keep=z4, Forward=z4+0.5(z4-z1), Adaptive=z4+[1-(top1(p4)-top2(p4))](z4-z1). The adaptive margin is over the entire token vocabulary. Negative=z4-0.5(z4-z1) and depths 1/2 are diagnostic fixed controls, not additional controller choices. Guidance precedes log-softmax at every candidate token.

## Splits and exposure
OpenBookQA official training pool is filtered against every historical question-bearing data manifest and exact normalized duplicates. Seed 2021 shuffle: first 400 discovery; next 3200 head-training, next 800 validation. No overlap by question text or ID. Official SciQ test and PIQA validation are final independent tasks, with all eligible questions and exclusion counts reported. Do not inspect final predictions or labels in analysis until policy lock. Manifest generation may read labels to serialize official targets but they cannot enter prediction features or policy selection. No claim about pretraining contamination follows from this exposure check.

SciQ's official harness prompt includes its supplied support paragraph. Do not call this a closed-book result. Probe targets are only tokens wholly inside the current question body, excluding the support paragraph, answer candidates, demonstration answers and reference solutions. Select at most 16 positions uniformly, using positions t-1 to predict token t. Known question words can incidentally coincide with an answer; selection must never consult the gold answer.

## Staged signal tests
Stage A reconstructs all four depth scores for 400 discovery questions after a 12-question correctness/cost pilot. Capture pre-output-norm last-prompt hidden states and fixed 32-dimensional random projections of three successive updates. These are distinct from earlier scalar magnitude features. Store only compact selected states and token statistics, not full vocabulary tensors.

Stage B fixed training-free rules: minimize known-question-token NLL among four actions; persistence sign chooses Forward for aligned final updates and Rollback for reversal, default Adaptive at zero; confidence selects the action with largest candidate probability margin; token-support selects the strongest mean candidate-token likelihood gain over Keep; two-format support selects the action with strongest minimum candidate margin across views. Ties retain Adaptive. Candidate probabilities are normalized likelihood weights, not calibrated answer probabilities. Analyse conditional predictive value against confidence, entropy, KL and depth prediction stability. Geometry and prompt-residual signs are fixed before outcomes; opposite signs may be reported only as exploratory.

Two views: official prompt and fixed `Q: question\nA:` formatting, preserving SciQ support. This supplies a perturbation, not independent factual evidence. Compare a two-view Adaptive ensemble using mean character-normalized candidate log likelihood, with exactly the same backbone passes. On final SciQ only, a third diagnostic removes support; compare support versus no-support accuracy and update value. This can probe the value of supplied evidence but cannot establish a universal causal evidence theorem or CoDR equivalence.

Stage C is allowed only after the training-free discovery report exists. Fit bounded linear ridge heads, never a larger classifier on old features alone: confidence controls, new geometry/probe features, and their combination. Ridge alpha=100 fixed; no architecture search. Predict each action's correctness delta relative to Adaptive on every training question, including neutral cases. Supplement with gold candidate log-weight gain as a separate target; do not conflate it with accuracy. Standardize using training statistics only; exclude candidate indices, gold labels, dataset identity, question ID, and raw question text. Maximum 2048 fitted scalar parameters per head. Require at least 50 positive and 50 negative pooled nondefault action events in training; otherwise report insufficient supervision and do not fit. Only one prescribed training size in this round; no indefinite tuning.

## Validation and policy lock
Compare the predeclared training-free rules and eligible heads on 800 held-out OpenBookQA training questions. Head action=argmax predicted gain, default Adaptive unless best gain>0.05. One global threshold, no action-specific thresholds. Validate candidate gains against Adaptive using paired bootstrap and exact McNemar. Lock at most one controller only if it gains at least 5 correct answers, has positive lower 95% paired bootstrap bound, and Holm-adjusted p<0.05 over candidate policies. Otherwise lock Adaptive as conservative default and final-evaluate predeclared diagnostic policies to measure generalization without claiming a successful controller.

Choose best fixed-depth control on validation only. Freeze model weights, feature names, policy and analysis script hashes before final inference. Primary final evidence is paired accuracy versus Adaptive and matched ensemble, with Holm correction across the two independent tasks and two comparisons. Also compare acceptance-rate-matched random actions (1000 shuffles within task), beneficial corrections retained/rejected, harmful changes avoided/accepted, rollback recovery and unreachable oracle errors. Oracle is an upper bound. AUC is secondary, on nonzero update events; do not reverse signs after looking at test outcomes.

Bootstrap: 10000 paired resamples, seed2021; report 95% percentile interval. Exact two-sided binomial McNemar on discordant pairs. Exploratory comparisons remain explicitly exploratory. No model transfer unless the locked Ouro controller beats both primary baselines with paired evidence; if successful, a separate Qwen protocol must precede any new Qwen inference.
