# Mechanism reading before implementation

Full HTML was read locally; URL, revision fetch hash and section headings are in paper_fetch_manifest.json. Full copyrighted text is not redistributed.

* [Training-Free Looped Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23872), sections2.1–2.3: forms F(g)=g(x)-x and substeps over a fixed unit interval; block and layer repetition are different. Euler/RK approximation quality is not an answer-quality certificate. Its wrapper cannot be silently substituted for native Ouro recurrence.
* [LoopCD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02185), sections3.1–3.2: contrasts reference and final states or logits; adaptive logits use the final full-vocabulary top-two probability gap. A head pass on the early state is real cost; confidence modulation does not directly measure update benefit.
* [TAPS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36653), section4.1, appendixB.3/D.1: EMA first/second moments separate persistent and fluctuating update energies, then map their balance to relaxation. Adjacent-update variants already use squared sums/differences. Relating these proxies to task-loss sensitivity requires proxy-alignment and temporal-mismatch assumptions. Alignment/curvature features alone are therefore neither new nor evidence of quality.
* [TaH2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35748), section4.2: online next-iteration target loss reductions provide continue labels, including no-gradient lookahead for stopped tokens. Positive-gain coverage and cost-sensitive BCE train a decider jointly with backbone/updater. Our frozen head differs in training scope but shares update-gain supervision; it is not automatically a novel mechanism.
* [Readout Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24136), section3.2/6: readout-dependent, normalized inter-token repulsion is gated by entropy/warmup and projected onto a latent manifold. Puzzle experiments do not validate language-model transfer; authors explicitly lack a reliable success diagnostic, and confident class separation can be wrong.
* [CoDR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08833), sections3.2–3.3/4.3: masked-diffusion commitments are re-probed under denser bidirectional context using stride partitions; dropped support triggers remask/refill. Token masking prevents self-conditioning. Ordinary causal prompt formatting is not this mechanism. Random remasking and additional-compute controls are essential.

Novelty standard: demonstrate incremental update-value information beyond these known statistics and beyond confidence, plus actual accuracy gains over Adaptive LoopCD and the matched ensemble. A null result warrants No-Go for this implementation family, not a universal impossibility claim.
