# Mechanism interpretation contract

This contract is an exploratory resource-allocation rule, not a confirmatory preregistration. It is written before outcomes of the accepted original-call causal screen. Old explicit-mask studies are engineering diagnostics and are not included in scientific summaries.

1. A correct-to-wrong native transition establishes harm from that complete transition on that question. It does not identify a shared harmful weight direction.
2. A normalized chord is a readout intervention. An interior margin above both endpoints, with improvement at the declared small positive step, is finite-sampled compatibility with overshoot. It is not a derivative estimate and does not uniquely separate H1 from H3.
3. Grid failure at0.125/0.25/0.5 does not prove that scalar contraction cannot recover correctness. The zero-step endpoint is the previously correct state; still smaller positive coefficients may recover it. Do not relabel such cases as established H2.
4. Feeding the loop5 chord to loop6 is a dynamics intervention. Persistence or disappearance of rescue distinguishes observed readout accessibility from tested downstream behavior, without identifying every possible hidden-information mechanism.
5. A post-branch-norm scalar lesion localizes execution sensitivity. It is not equivalent to scaling the raw output projection: Ouro applies branch RMSNorm after that matrix, which approximately cancels positive uniform matrix scaling. A fifth-loop-only lesion is also not a shared physical weight edit.
6. The48-module screen uses eight selected discovery representatives. Its best module is selected from a search and does not have an independent test-set significance interpretation. Full results, including stable-correct and native-rescue regressions, must be preserved.
7. At most two modules warrant additional shared-invocation confirmation only when local net correctness improves, neither native-rescue nor stable-correct representatives regress, and the effect exceeds the tested boundary controls under the analyzer's declared rule. Added grid-unresolved harms are explicitly exploratory followup cases.
8. Only a transferable, non-collinear, norm-controlled component intervention beyond scalar/random controls would justify a shared weight candidate. PCA concentration, large branch norms, and successful scalar lesions are insufficient on their own. Any PCA/SVD direction must be defined without supervised parameter fitting and before held-out selection feedback.
9. If these causal gates do not justify a direction, stop the current H2 weight-editing path. A no-edit manifest is the correct deliverable. This means failure to establish the proposed mechanism within the bounded study, not proof that every possible harmful component or repair is absent.
10. Official validation and the1172-question test may evaluate only frozen methods. The test is a locked regression benchmark previously used in earlier work, never a claimed new blind benchmark. No post-test method, coefficient, depth or direction selection is permitted.
