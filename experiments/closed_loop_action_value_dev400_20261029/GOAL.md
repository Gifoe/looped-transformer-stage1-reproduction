# Goal and completion boundary

The overall objective remains: discover an intermediate signal of recurrent update value in a frozen model, prefer a training-free controller, permit only a bounded lightweight head if needed, use it to control continuation/rollback/extrapolation, and verify reliable benefit over LoopCD on independent tasks.

The user clarified that an informative and causally useful intermediate variable is the central mechanism; baseline superiority remains important, not the sole scientific question. This study therefore tests signal information and actual state intervention separately, without redefining the full goal as implementation success.

Evidence ladder:

1. The sealed eight-question pilot establishes a functioning state-feedback interface only.
2. This locked 400-question exposed-development study measures proxy information, paired corrections/degradations and selection specificity against fixed, confidence, shuffled and terminal-only controls.
3. A future selected controller still requires untouched confirmation, task-specific scoring, and competitive end-to-end cost comparisons, including an appropriate equal-compute alternative when extra prompt passes are used. This study cannot establish that final claim.

No modification of model weights, hidden use of gold at inference, repeated final-test selection, or silent replacement of prediction quality with proxy loss is permitted. A measured negative result is retained. The overall goal remains unachieved until its mechanism and independent efficacy requirements are actually supported.
